# Enthusiast

**Persona:** Enthusiast  
**Depth default:** Advanced

Someone who already likes the table and wants the instrument, not the exam sheet.

## Job

Home asks: *What is interesting here beyond the line I memorized?*

Success: they can move from a tile, to the reason a trend breaks, to a line and an isotope, and find that place again on this device through Recents and bookmarks.

## A sitting

```text
Matrix on a wide window
  → wander a family by chip
  → open gold or uranium because the card looked good
  → stay on Advanced
  → Spectra, scrub a line, play the transposed tone
  → read which isotopes are stable and which are synthetic
  → save a comparison that is not a textbook pair
  → come back tomorrow through Recents
```

Element of the day is a suggestion, easy to ignore. Bookmarks and saved pairs are the memory.

## What we owe them

- Advanced is not a wall of unlabeled constants. Each value keeps its name and unit.
- Missing trend essays stay missing. A serious reader trusts a blank more than a generated paragraph.
- Source pins are easy to find.
- Theme and glass are theirs to tune. The family colors are not.

## What this is not

A research notebook, a personal database of assays, or a place to correct NIST in public. Corrections belong upstream. Element Deck updates by shipping a new catalog release.
