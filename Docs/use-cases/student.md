# Student

**Persona:** Student  
**Depth default:** Intermediate  
**Rooms:** [App UI](../app-ui.md) · [Learning](../learning.md)

A student uses Element Deck to **understand an element**, not to grind a streak of symbols.

## Job

Home asks: *What should I understand about this element today?*

Success: they can place it on the table, say what it is like at their depth, and explain one difference from a neighbor. Neon should also mean a color, not only a noble-gas cell.

## A sitting

```text
Open Table
  → element of the day, or search "Ti"
  → read the card at Intermediate
  → Explore Orbitals
  → scan metrics, phase, one application
  → if lost, switch to Beginner and read the curriculum card
  → Compare with a suggested neighbor
  → Spectra if the chapter mentions light or isotopes
  → bookmark
```

The sitting works on a phone between classes. The preview needs no account. The full table needs the Element Deck package, downloaded once and then available offline. A bookmark is written on the device and copied to the account when they are signed in.

## Depth in practice

| Moment | Depth |
| --- | --- |
| First week of the topic | Beginner: state, shells, a use |
| Writing the exam sheet | Intermediate: configuration, oxidation, the numbers |
| A question they cannot answer from the sheet | Advanced, for that element only |

Switching back is normal. The app does not congratulate them for staying on Advanced.

## Carbon and silicon

This is the comparison the concept screen teaches. A student in a bonding chapter should be able to finish the sitting and say: carbon is smaller and holds a covalent network more tightly; silicon is larger, the bonds are weaker, and the material behaves as a semiconductor. The bars and the trend paragraph carry that. The PDF is what they keep.

## What we owe them

- Numbers with units, and a visible dataset pin when they go looking (Spectra footer, PDF footer, About).
- "Not reported" instead of a zero.
- A reaction button only when the illustration is real.
- A tone that admits it was transposed.
- No account wall in front of the preview. The full pack is a purchase, then a download, then ordinary offline reading.

## Out of this story

Quizzes, leaderboards, and a chat that answers "explain titanium" in a paragraph the catalog already contains. The dossier is that paragraph, at the depth they chose.
