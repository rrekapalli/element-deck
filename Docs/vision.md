# Vision

## The sentence

**A periodic-table instrument that teaches every element at the depth the learner is ready for.**

The original intention: one explorer for the whole table — phone in a pocket, tablet or browser on a desk — where atomic number, comparison, and light are the same object seen from different rooms.

## The category error to avoid

The mistake would be to build a poster of the periodic table, or a Wikipedia page with a dark theme.

Poster apps optimize recognition of the grid. Encyclopedia apps optimize a long article. Quiz apps optimize recall of symbols. Lab notebooks optimize the user's own measurements.

Element Deck optimizes **learning the element**.

| Familiar tools start from | Element Deck starts from |
| --- | --- |
| Here is the grid. Find the symbol. | Here is the element. What should you understand about it at your depth? |
| Here is an article. Read everything. | Here are the facts that matter now, with the rest one tap deeper. |
| Memorize Z, symbol, and mass. | See why carbon and silicon diverge, and hear the light an element emits. |
| A static chart for the wall. | The same chart on a phone, and a full bench on a tablet or the web. |

## One catalog, four rooms, three depths

Every element is one record. The app does not fork into a "kids table" and a "scientists table."

```text
TABLE        where it sits, and what family it belongs to
DETAILS      what it is, at the depth you chose
COMPARE      how it differs from its neighbor
SPECTRA      the light it makes, and the isotopes behind that atom
```

Depth is a lens on that record:

```text
Beginner       shells, everyday state, what people use it for
Intermediate   configuration, oxidation, lattice, the main numbers
Advanced       quantum metrics, spectral lines, isotopes, why the trend breaks
```

The concept screens already show this. Table and Details carry a **Complexity** control (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced). Compare and Spectra are the same element seen as a relationship and as light.

## What makes it different

- **Element-centric.** Atomic number is the identity. Articles, tiles, and spectra are views.
- **Depth-aware.** The same titanium dossier teaches a school student and an undergraduate without a second app.
- **Comparative.** Neighbors are first-class. Group 14 carbon versus silicon is a teaching object, not a coincidence of two pages.
- **Spectral.** Emission, absorption, glow, and isotopes sit in their own room, cited, not buried in a footnote.
- **Honest about numbers.** A value shows its quantity, unit, and source. A sonified frequency says it was transposed into hearing range. A reaction simulation says it is an illustration.
- **Instrument, not cartoon.** Obsidian Orbit: dark glass, monospace measurements, category color that means a real family.
- **Two form factors, one product.** Phone navigation for one-handed lookup. Tablet and web for the matrix, the split dossier, and a classroom desk.

## The four layers

```text
LEARNER
Student / Educator / Enthusiast
        ↓
DEPTH
Beginner • Intermediate • Advanced
        ↓
ELEMENT
Identity • Properties • Isotopes • Spectrum • Uses • Discovery
        ↓
ROOMS
Table • Details • Compare • Spectra
```

The catalog is the data. Depth is how much of it is in front of the learner. The rooms are how they walk it. Settings and theme change the instrument's chrome. They do not recolor the science.

## Why comparison and spectra are rooms

A table that only opens an article teaches location. Students get stuck on *why this neighbor is different* and *what the colored lines mean*.

Compare exists so a congener pair (carbon and silicon in the concept) can show radius, electronegativity, ionization, melting point, density, and valence side by side, then explain the trend break in prose.

Spectra exists so neon is not only a noble-gas tile. It is a discharge color, a wavelength, a transposed tone, an astrophysical identification, and a set of isotopes with half-lives.

Those two rooms are part of v1. They are not extras behind the article.

## Naming

**Element Deck** is the product name. Docs, UI chrome, and package names say **Element Deck**.

The concept screens still show an older wordmark, **ATOMIQ**. Treat those PNGs as the layout and visual system. Replace the wordmark when the shell is built. The repository name is `element-deck`.
