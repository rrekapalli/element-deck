# App Config

![App Config](asset:assets/help/images/app_settings.png)

Callouts: **learning mode** · **appearance** · **persona** · **units** · **sound and motion**.

## Purpose

App Config is how Element Deck looks, reads, and sounds on this device. Open it from **Settings**. Nothing here changes the stored scientific values. Display units and the depth of the writing change. The dataset stays as published.

These choices are saved on the device immediately. When you are signed in, the same preferences copy to the account.

## Learning mode

**Beginner**, **Intermediate**, and **Advanced** change how much terminology the rooms use.

- **Beginner** stays with shells, everyday state, and uses. On Spectra, listen reads **Play the color as a tone**.
- **Intermediate** is mass, shells, and oxidation, and is the usual reading level.
- **Advanced** keeps quantum metrics, line assignments, and isotope detail in view. On Spectra the selected line shows its transition.

The caption under the control states which mode is active. The same mode applies in every room.

## Appearance

**Light**, **Dark**, and **System** set the theme. **System** follows the device.

The color chips are the chrome themes: Obsidian Orbit and the other presets. Family colors on element tiles do not change with the chrome theme. A tile's category name is always printed next to the color.

**Frosted glass** turns the translucent bars on or off. **Reduce glow** quiets extra glow on the theme.

## Persona

**Student**, **Educator**, and **Enthusiast** record who the device is set up for. The four rooms stay the same. Persona does not hide elements or replace the navigation.

## Units

Each switch changes display only:

- **Temperature in °C** — off shows kelvin.
- **Energy in kJ/mol** — off shows electronvolts.
- **Length in Å** — off shows picometres.
- **Noble-gas shorthand** — on shows configurations such as `[He] 2s2 2p2`. Off prefers the expanded shell list where the dossier prints both.

Details, Compare, and Spectra all follow these switches.

## Sound and motion

**Sonification** allows the Spectra **Listen** button to play a tone. Off, that button explains that sound is off.

**Reduced motion** shortens or removes motion, including the electron diagram and the jump to a spectrum essay.

## Try this

1. Set learning mode to **Beginner**, open Spectra, and read the listen button.
2. Switch it back to **Intermediate**.
3. Turn **Temperature in °C** on and check a melting point in Details → Data.
4. Turn **Sonification** off if the room should stay silent, then on again when you want the tone.
