# Details

![Details](asset:assets/help/images/details.png)

Callouts: **identity header** · **bookmark** · **Details, Data, History, Uses**.

## Purpose

Details is the dossier for the current element. It opens from a table cell, a registry card, or the **Details** tab. If you have not chosen an element yet, the dossier uses the element already selected for the session.

The header line reads **PERIODIC EXPLORER · DETAILS**.

## Identity header

The header stays pinned while the page scrolls.

- A short isotope line, when the pack has isotopes for this element.
- The symbol, in the family color, with atomic number and name.
- Atomic weight and whether that weight is a standard atomic weight or a representative isotope.
- Category, block, period, and group.
- **Bookmark**. A filled bookmark means this element is saved on this device. Tap it again to remove the bookmark.

An element that is not in the on-device catalog shows its name and the note **In the full pack**.

## Details tab

The first tab is a reading page for this element. The wording follows **Learning mode** in App Config:

- **Beginner** is an introduction, the kind of explanation a class meets when an element is new.
- **Intermediate** adds the period, the configuration, and the oxidation states.
- **Advanced** is written for later secondary school: configuration, oxidation, and why the numbers look that way.

Only the first two sentences sit above the electron picture. The rest of the explanation is below it.

- A shell diagram of the electrons. **Reduced motion** in App Config turns off extra motion in that diagram. Beginner hides the energy numbers on that diagram.
- **Simulate Reaction**, when a known reaction is curated. It opens an illustration: reactants, products, conditions, and a teaching note at the current learning mode. It is an illustration of a published reaction, not a lab procedure to run.

## Data tab

**Data** lists compiled values in labeled groups: identity, electrons, physical properties, isotopes, and spectrum.

Units follow **App Config**. A temperature can display in °C while the stored value stays in kelvin. The same is true for energy and length.

Labels with a dotted underline open a reference article for that property: what it measures and where the unit comes from. The article is about the property, not a second measurement of this element.

Missing values say **Not reported**. They are not filled in with a guess.

## History tab

**History** is a narrative for this element, plus a discovery card when the pack has a year, the people, and a short genesis note. If the pack has no history yet, the page says so.

**Trivia** is at the bottom of the same tab. Each card is a short story. Tap a card to read it.

## Uses tab

**Uses** opens with a picture of the element itself. Most cards show the pure element, or the small sample a laboratory actually keeps. The credit under the picture names the photographer and the license. A few elements have never been gathered into a visible piece. Those cards say so, and they do not invent a lump of metal.

Below that, the tab lists what people do with the element. A use may include its own photograph. The credit line names the source. A use with no picture is text only.

## Try this

1. Open carbon from Table, then switch **Details**, **Data**, **History**, and **Uses**.
2. On **Details**, change **Learning mode** and read the sentences above the picture again.
3. On **Data**, tap **Electronegativity** and read the property article. Back returns to the dossier.
4. On **History**, scroll to **Trivia** and open a story.
5. Bookmark the element and confirm the icon fills.
6. If **Simulate Reaction** is shown, open it, read the equation, and dismiss the sheet.
