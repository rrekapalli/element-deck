![About](asset:assets/help/images/about.png)

Callouts: **mark** · **name** · **disclaimer**.

## Purpose

About is the short statement of what Element Deck is. Open it from **Settings**.

The page shows the app mark, the name **Element Deck**, and this disclaimer:

> Element Deck explains published elemental data for learning. It is not a lab measurement, a specification for engineering or medicine, or a substitute for the cited dataset.

The same sentence closes every article in this guide. Spectra names the dataset on its own page. A comparison PDF repeats that the page is teaching copy.

## What the app is not

Element Deck is not a feed, a streak, or a points game. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or certify a material for engineering. Numbers on Details, Compare, and Spectra are the published values in the on-device pack, displayed in the units you chose.

## Try this

1. Open About from Settings and read the disclaimer once.
2. Open Spectra and find the dataset name in the emitter row, so you can see which list the lines came from.
3. Tap **Previous** to return to App Health.
