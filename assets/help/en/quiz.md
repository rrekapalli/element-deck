# Quiz

Callouts: **today's element** · **learning mode** · **50-question pool** · **XP and streak**.

## Purpose

Quiz is practice for one element at the current learning mode. The header reads **PERIODIC EXPLORER · QUIZ**. Opening the room focuses **today's element**, the same daily pick as Table, then asks from a 50-question pool.

Table, Details, Compare, and Spectra do not keep score. Quiz does, on this device only.

## Learning mode

Quiz uses the app learning mode from **App Config** (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced). This room does not have its own mode switch. Each mode has its own 50 questions. Changing the mode in settings loads that pool and leaves the other pools' answers in place.

## Answering

Each item has four choices. After you pick one, the correct option is marked and a short explanation appears. An equation line appears only when the catalog already has that reaction or value.

**Previous** and **Next** move through all 50 questions. You can skip a question and come back. Answering is not required to move.

A correct answer is worth **20 XP**. XP is twenty times the number of correct answers across every element and mode. A wrong answer records the miss and adds no XP. You cannot retake the same item to change the score.

## Streak and tier

**Streak** counts consecutive local calendar days with at least one answer. Skip a day, and the next answer starts the streak at one.

**Tier** counts finished pools (all 50 questions answered), not XP:

- Observer
- Student (1–4 pools)
- Scholar (5–14)
- Fellow (15–39)
- Master (40 or more)

## Switching elements

Chips under the bank change Quiz focus: today, the element open in Details, and recent elements. They do not change the Details dossier by themselves.

**Notes** are private text for this element. **Bookmark** is the same bookmark as Details. **Report** stores a flag on this device; nothing is uploaded.

## The bank

Questions are written from the catalog: identity, electrons, nuclei, measured properties, uses when the pack has them, discovery, and light or neighbor comparisons. Missing industrial uses are not invented for synthetic elements. Numbers in the correct choice come from that element's record.
