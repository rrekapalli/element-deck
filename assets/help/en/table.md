# Table

![Table](asset:assets/help/images/table.png)

Callouts: **Search** · **family chips** · **periodic table** · **registry list**.

## Purpose

Table is the home of the explorer. Search the elements, narrow them by family, see where they sit on the periodic table, and open a dossier from the registry.

The header shows **Element Deck** and **PERIODIC EXPLORER · TABLE**. **?** opens this guide. The person icon opens **Settings**.

## Search

The field accepts a name, a symbol, or an atomic number.

- A number jumps to that atomic number (Z).
- A symbol prefers an exact match, such as `Na` or `Fe`.
- A name matches as you type.

If nothing matches, the registry is empty. Clear the search text to see the family again. The app does not silently substitute a nearby element.

## Family chips

The chips sit under search. Each one shows how many elements it selects, as in **All (118)** or **Reactive Nonmetals**.

**All** clears the family filter. The other chips are the families: reactive nonmetals, noble gases, alkali, alkaline earth, transition, post-transition, metalloids, lanthanides, actinides, and halogens.

**Halogens** is a tag, so it can overlap the reactive-nonmetal family. The colored dot on a chip is the family color. The name is always printed next to it.

The filter stays until you pick another chip.

## Periodic table

On a phone the table sits above the registry. Tap a cell to open that element's **Details**. Cells outside the active family are dimmed, so the filter is visible on the table and in the list together.

Lanthanides and actinides are called out in their own rows.

## Registry

**Atomic Registry** counts how many elements the current search and chip leave visible, for example **Atomic Registry · 13 shown**.

Each card shows the atomic number, the symbol in the family color, the name, the category and standard state, and the atomic weight. Tap a card to open **Details** for that element. The card you open becomes the current element for Details, Compare, and Spectra.

## Bottom navigation

**Table**, **Details**, **Compare**, and **Spectra** switch rooms. The current element is kept. Tap **Table** again while you are already here to return to the top of the room.

## Try this

1. Search `6`, then clear it and search `carbon`.
2. Tap **Reactive Nonmetals** and compare the count on the chip with **Atomic Registry**.
3. Tap **Carbon** in the registry and confirm **Details** opens on carbon.
4. Return with the **Table** tab. The chip you chose is still selected.
