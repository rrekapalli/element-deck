# Spectra

![Spectra](asset:assets/help/images/spectra.png)

Callouts: **target emitter** · **emission or absorption** · **visible strip** · **listen** · **isotopes**.

## Purpose

Spectra is the bench for the current element: its lines, a sonified tone, the discharge color when we have one, and its isotopes. The header reads **PERIODIC EXPLORER · SPECTRA**.

**Dataset** names the bundled line list this screen was built against. "Calibrated" here means that list, not that the phone is a spectrometer.

## Target emitter

Chips across the top are the current element and elements you opened recently. The label is the symbol and atomic number, such as `C #6`. Choosing a chip updates the current element for Details and Table as well.

## Emission and absorption

**Emission** and **Absorption** filter the stored lines. A line marked for both spectra stays visible in either mode.

## The strip

The colored strip is the visible window, about **380–780 nm**. Lines in that window are drawn on it. Tap the strip, or drag the slider, to move the selection onto the nearest stored line. The control does not invent a line between the dataset's lines.

The caption under the strip counts how many of this element's lines fall in the window. Lines outside the window are listed below and marked **outside 380–780 nm**. If the pack has no lines, the strip says so.

## A selected line

The line list shows wavelength, the transition or color, and energy in eV. Tap a row to select it.

Under the list, the selection restates wavelength, frequency in terahertz, color, and energy. In **Advanced** learning mode the transition assignment stays visible with the selection.

## Listen

**Listen** plays the selected line's frequency scaled into the audible range. The button keeps the real frequency and the word **sonified**, except in Beginner mode, where it reads **Play the color as a tone**.

The tone is a teaching stand-in. It is not the light you would hear, and light is not sound. Turn **Sonification** off in **App Config** and the button reads **Sound is off in App Config**.

## Glow, sky, and isotopes

**Characteristic Glow** is the discharge color when the pack has one, with a short note and a voltage figure where we have it. That color can differ from the family color used on table tiles.

**Astrophysical match** is a sourced note, such as a stellar or solar line, when we have one. The page does not show match percentages.

**Isotopes & decay modes** lists mass number, neutron count, mass, spin, abundance or half-life, and a stability word such as stable, trace, or synthetic.

The footer names the pack version and reminds you that the noble-gas tile color is not the discharge glow.

## Details tab

The second tab, **Details**, is a reading page: a primer on spectra, then an essay for this element when one is written. **Jump to {element}** scrolls to that essay. Line values on this tab can open the same property articles as the Data tab in Details.

## Try this

1. Open neon, or another element with several visible lines, from the emitter chips.
2. Switch **Absorption** and back to **Emission** and watch which rows remain.
3. Drag the slider and confirm the selection lands on a listed wavelength.
4. Tap **Listen** once. If the room is meant to stay quiet, turn sonification off in App Config first.
