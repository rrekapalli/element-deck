# Account settings

![Account settings](asset:assets/help/images/account_settings.png)

Callouts: **sign-in** · **packages** · **content pack** · **export** · **erase**.

## Purpose

Account settings is the buyer, their packages, and this device's copy of their data. Open it from **Settings**.

## Sign-in

Signed out, the page says **Not signed in** and offers **Sign in with Google**. The preview catalog still opens without an account. Buying, restoring, and downloading the full pack need a signed-in account.

Signed in, the page shows the name, the email, and **Signed in with Google**.

## Packages

**Packages** lists what this account owns. An empty purchase list reads **Preview only**.

- **Buy Element Deck** starts the store purchase for the full pack.
- **Restore purchases** asks the store to return purchases already made with this account.

A message from the store or from sign-in appears at the bottom of the page when there is one.

## Content pack

**Download content pack** fetches the full pack when the account is entitled to it, then reloads the catalog. The subtitle shows the download status or the pack version already stored.

Until that download finishes, rooms keep using the preview catalog.

## Your data

**Export my learner data** shares a JSON file of preferences, bookmarks, and saved comparisons from this device. Purchases stay with the store and with Firebase. They are not in that file.

**Sign out** keeps the local cache. The pack stays on the device until you erase it.

**Erase this device** asks you to type `DELETE`. That clears the learner database and the pack cache on this device. It does not refund a purchase and it does not delete the purchase record.

## Try this

1. Read the sign-in row and note whether this device is on the preview catalog.
2. Open **Packages** and see whether it says **Preview only**.
3. Export learner data and confirm the share sheet opens. You can dismiss it.
4. Leave **Erase this device** unused unless you intend to clear this device.
